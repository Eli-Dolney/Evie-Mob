LTW MOBS BEDROCK 0.1.0 — FIRST PC TEST

This is a test build for Evie the Dragon and Eli, King of the Seas.
It contains their updated models, animations, spawn eggs, taming, saddles,
follow/stay, flight, swimming, and companion protection.

INSTALL ON WINDOWS

1. Update and open Minecraft for Windows (Bedrock), not Minecraft Java.
   Minimum supported game version: 1.21.90.
2. Download ltw-mobs-bedrock-0.1.0.mcaddon onto your Windows PC.
3. Double-click it, or choose Open with > Minecraft.
4. Wait for the two packs to import.
5. Create a NEW Creative test world with a wide open land area and a deep ocean.
6. In the world's Add-Ons settings, open Behavior Packs > My Packs and activate
   "LTW Mobs Bedrock — Behaviors".
7. Check that "LTW Mobs Bedrock — Creatures" is also active under Resource Packs.
   Activate it manually if needed. You need both packs.
8. Leave experiments OFF. This pack is designed for the normal release game.
9. Enter the world. Search the Creative inventory for Evie and Eli spawn eggs.
   Spawn Evie on open ground and Eli in deep, open water.

Optional commands (require cheats):
  /summon ltw_mobs:evie
  /summon ltw_mobs:eli

TAMING AND RIDING

Evie: Feed her glow berries five times.
Eli: Feed him tropical fish five times.
Hearts and a Trust: n/5 message show progress.
If a different player feeds an untamed creature, their trust starts at one.
Creative feeding keeps your items. Survival feeding consumes one each time.

After taming, interact while holding a saddle to equip it.
Select an empty hotbar slot, then right-click the creature to mount.
Only the owner can saddle, command, or ride it. Each carries one rider.

EVIE
  On land: use normal movement controls.
  Press Jump (normally Space) to take off.
  Move forward and look where you want to fly.
  Look upward to climb; look downward to descend.
  Release movement to hover.
  Descend onto open ground to land.

ELI
  Use normal movement controls and look where you want to swim.
  Look down and move forward to dive; look up to surface.
  Movement stops at dry boundaries or blocked water passages.
  Use a broad, deep ocean for the first test: his collision box is 3 blocks wide.
  He waits when following would take him onto land.

FOLLOW / STAY
  While unmounted, empty your hand, crouch (normally Shift), and right-click.
  This toggles follow/stay. Eli follows while you are in water.

DISMOUNT
  Use Minecraft's normal dismount control (normally Shift).
  Leaving Evie in flight grants slow falling.
  Leaving Eli underwater grants 60 seconds of water breathing.
  Eli provides water breathing and night vision while ridden underwater.

Tamed companions are protected from normal damage in this test version.
Custom islands, natural spawning, locators, boosting, and the 30-second
recovery mechanic are not included yet.

IF SOMETHING DOES NOT WORK

Send the exact Minecraft version and what failed, plus a screenshot or short
video if possible. For script errors, enable the Content Log GUI/file in
Minecraft's Settings > Creator settings, then reproduce the problem and send
the first LTW Mobs error. Also check that both packs are active.

WHAT HAS BEEN CHECKED

The scripts compile against the stable @minecraft/server 2.0.0 API.
Eleven logic tests cover taming, food use, ownership, saved state, and swimming
input. Pack references, manifest dependencies, all 186 model pieces, animation
bone names, and face UVs have been checked. Both creature behavior definitions
pass Mojang's published component schemas, with a documented upstream schema
filename correction in the validation tool used during development.

Windows Bedrock was not available on the development Mac. Importing into
Minecraft, visual appearance, actual touch/controller input, collision feel,
and multiplayer have NOT yet been verified in-game. This is ready for your
first PC test, not a confirmed finished port.

IPAD AND PS5 — AFTER THE PC TEST

The controls use standard movement, looking, interaction, and Jump, so the
same pack is designed for touch and controller use. Test iPad with your PC's
multiplayer world next. After those tests, upload the Add-On-enabled world
from Windows to a Bedrock Realm and join from iPad or PS5.

EDITABLE SOURCE

ltw-mobs-bedrock-0.1.0-source.zip includes both packs, original Blockbench
models, textures, TypeScript scripts, build tools, and the focused tests.
The original Java mod and its supplied ZIP have not been modified.
