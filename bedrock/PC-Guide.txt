LTW MOBS BEDROCK 0.2.0 — HABITATS AND COMPANIONS TEST

Added: Evie's floating cherry island, Eli's tropical lagoon, one wild companion
per habitat, craftable locators, boosting, healing, and optional 30-second rest.
The earlier models, animations, taming, saddles, riding, and protection remain.

INSTALL ON WINDOWS

1. Update Minecraft for Windows (Bedrock). Minimum: 1.21.90.
2. Download ltw-mobs-bedrock-0.2.0.mcaddon and double-click it to import.
3. Create a NEW Creative test world, with cheats ON for the quick checks below.
4. Activate "LTW Mobs Bedrock — Behaviors" in Behavior Packs > My Packs.
5. Ensure "LTW Mobs Bedrock — Creatures" is active under Resource Packs too.
6. Keep experiments OFF. Both packs are needed.
7. Enter the world. Enable Content Log in Settings > Creator if diagnosing errors.

0.2.0 keeps the earlier pack identities and is intended to upgrade 0.1.0 saved
companions. Back up any earlier test world before upgrading it. Only one version
of each pack should be active. Use a fresh world to test the new generation.
The original Java mod and the older downloadable 0.1.0 build are preserved.

FIND THEIR HOMES

Evie: floating islands over Overworld land, with cherry trees, flowers, crystals,
and a waterfall. Eli: deep lagoons with a sandy tropical ring and a wide opening
to warm/lukewarm oceans. They are rare placement attempts in NEW terrain only.
One companion is assigned to each home. Taming or leaving does not spawn another.
A killed wild companion is not automatically replaced.

Sky Island Locator: craft a compass + an amethyst shard at a crafting table.
Sea Lagoon Locator: craft a compass + a prismarine shard at a crafting table.
They also appear in Creative. Hold one to see direction, distance, coordinates;
use it to refresh the message. A locator finds the nearest DISCOVERED habitat,
shared across players. It cannot locate homes in unexplored terrain. It keeps
pointing to the home after its companion leaves. Discovery requires the habitat
crystal to load inside simulation distance. Do not copy/move these crystals.

QUICK HABITAT TEST — WITHOUT SEARCHING FOR RARE SPAWNS

In the new disposable Creative world, enter these commands in chat. Structure
loading replaces blocks in its footprint: use these only away from your builds.

  /structure load ltw_mobs:sky_island 100 110 100
  /tp @s 123 131 123

Fly down to the central crystal. Wait a few seconds for Evie to appear.
The island is 47 x 28 x 47 blocks; its deck is at Y=126.

  /structure load ltw_mobs:sea_lagoon 200 44 200
  /tp @s 231 66 231

Eli should appear in the center water, near Y=58. The lagoon is 63 x 30 x 63.
Stay nearby so the crystal at Y=46 loads. Check both appear once after reloading.
These commands test templates and spawning, NOT natural generation. For that,
explore fresh land and warm/lukewarm ocean chunks and look for naturally placed
habitats. No /locate command exists for these custom features.

  /give @s ltw_mobs:sky_locator
  /give @s ltw_mobs:sea_locator

Creative eggs and /summon ltw_mobs:evie or /summon ltw_mobs:eli still work.

CARE AND CONTROLS

Tame Evie with five glow berries; tame Eli with five tropical fish. Hearts and
Trust: n/5 show progress. A new feeder starts their own progress at one.
Survival consumes one item per feed; Creative keeps it.
Only the owner can add a saddle, command, heal, or ride. Each carries one rider.
Add a saddle, select an EMPTY hand, then interact to mount.
Unmounted: crouch + empty-hand interact toggles follow/stay.
Eli follows through reachable water and waits when you are on land or blocked.

Evie: normal movement on land. Jump to take off. Move forward and look where
you want to fly. Release movement to hover; descend onto open ground to land.
Eli: move and look where you want to swim. Look down to dive and up to surface.
Use a broad deep area; his body is three blocks wide. He stops before dry land.

Boost: Sprint for 1.6x speed if your riding controls pass Sprint through.
Alternatively, after mounting, hold the matching locator and USE it for a
five-second boost. It recharges ten seconds after activation. Use the standard
item-use control on PC, touch, or controller. No custom keyboard keys are used.

Dismount normally. Leaving airborne Evie grants slow falling. Leaving underwater
Eli grants 60 seconds of water breathing. Eli gives underwater water breathing
and night vision while riding. Disconnecting stops riding/boost movement.

PROTECTION AND OPTIONAL RECOVERY

Your default choice remains: tamed companions ignore normal damage.
To try the Java-style damage + recovery mode, an operator can enter:

  /scriptevent ltw_mobs:protection recovery

The setting applies to all tamed companions and persists after reloads. Their
play health is 100. Feed their usual food to heal 12 points. A fatal ordinary hit
ejects the rider safely and starts 30 seconds of protected rest, then full health
returns. Rest pauses when unloaded/world closed; commands and riding are blocked.
Watch the action-bar health text: Bedrock mount hearts may show the separate
native health reserve. Extreme damage and /kill are outside recovery protection.

Test the timer on your mount, or nearest owned companion within 12 blocks:
  /scriptevent ltw_mobs:recover test

Restore the original damage immunity:
  /scriptevent ltw_mobs:protection immune
